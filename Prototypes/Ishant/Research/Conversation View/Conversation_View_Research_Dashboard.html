About this Research & Dataset

Source File:
/Users/ishant.p/Documents/Titan Design Git/Prototypes/Ishant/Research/Conversation View/Conversation_View_Feedback_Classification.xlsx

Data Scope: 757 verified customer feedback comments collected from NPS and customer touchpoints across Titan partners (WordPress.com, Hostinger, HostGator, Secureserver, Name.com, etc.) from October 2020 through August 2026.

Methodology: Every feedback text was reviewed and categorized against 13 specific UX sub-themes with highlighted excerpts. In addition, an unbiased deep-dive on the 297 unclassified records revealed key functional user needs such as single-message forwarding confidentiality, mobile performance, and thread-level search collisions.
