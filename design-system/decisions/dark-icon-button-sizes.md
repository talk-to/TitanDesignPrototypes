# Dark Icon button sizes

User-requested size configurations within the existing Dark treatment: 24px (legacy default), 32px and 40px targets; default artwork is 16px, 20px and 24px respectively. Explicit iconSize remains independent. Padding is zero, artwork is centered, and parent gaps remain caller-owned. All configurations share existing Dark interaction and disabled styling. No new variant family.

Canonical previews and interaction targets group the three sizes consecutively. Calendar, Contacts and Drive copies in the App sidebar prototype consume size:32; their local width/height overrides were removed. Dark quiet remains unchanged.
